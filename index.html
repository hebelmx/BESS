The Opportunity

Mexico's DAC tariff (Domestica de Alto Consumo) is a punitive, unsubsidized electricity rate that charges $5.45-$6.92 MXN/kWh (~$0.30-$0.38 USD) — roughly 4-6x the subsidized rate.

Once triggered by exceeding just 250 kWh/month (rolling 12-month average in the Bajio), DAC applies to all consumption, not just the excess. This creates monthly bills of $3,000-$12,000+ MXN for upper-income households.

Combined with grid instability and post-blackout voltage spikes destroying electronics, the value proposition extends far beyond energy savings into equipment protection and operational resilience.

💡
The CRE formalized BESS integration rules in March 2025, targeting 8,412 MW of storage deployment nationally by 2038. The residential segment remains nascent — no dominant player exists in the Bajio.

The Product Sweet Spot

What the Mexican market actually needs — designed from the market backward, not from the factory forward.

Must-Have Features for Mexico

  • ✓ Built-in voltage conditioning — Not just backup, but active power quality management. This is THE differentiator. Mexican homes need clean, stable 127V/60Hz output regardless of grid input.
  • ✓ Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) — Seamless <20ms switchover. The family shouldn't notice the blackout happened.
  • ✓ Surge suppression on input — Absorb post-blackout voltage spikes (150-180V) before they reach the home. This alone justifies the product.
  • ✓ 10-15 kWh capacity — Sweet spot for Bajio DAC households. Enough for 4-8 hours of essential loads (refrigerator, A/C, lights, internet, security).
  • ✓ 5 kW continuous / 10 kW peak — Handles A/C startup surges. Underpowered systems lose the A/C market — and in Bajio summers (35-40°C), A/C is non-negotiable.
  • ✓ Hybrid inverter included — Solar-ready from day one. Turnkey solution, not a component sale.
  • ✓ Tropical climate rating — Operating temp up to 50°C ambient. IP65 minimum. Bajio summers are brutal on outdoor equipment.
  • ✓ App in Spanish (Freemium + Premium) — Layered app model. Free tier drives adoption and referrals. Premium subscription unlocks advanced analytics and predictive diagnostics. See App Platform below.
  • ✓ LFP chemistry — 6,000+ cycles, no thermal runaway risk, no cobalt supply chain issues.
